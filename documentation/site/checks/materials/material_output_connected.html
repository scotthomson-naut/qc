Materials check

Material Output Connected

Checks materials for shader nodes that are not connected, directly or indirectly, to an active Material Output node.

WarningAutomatic fix: NoSettings: YesSelection: Object

Why this check exists

This check protects scene quality and helps catch production issues before publishing or rendering.

Behavior

Modulematerials/material_output_connected.py
