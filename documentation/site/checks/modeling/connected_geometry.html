Modeling check

Connected Geometry

Checks mesh objects for vertices, edges, or faces that are not connected to the object's main mesh body.

WarningAutomatic fix: YesSettings: YesSelection: Vertices, edges and faces

Why this check exists

This check protects scene quality and helps catch production issues before publishing or rendering.
